## Who processes the data

The personal data operator is SmartSol.

- Email: [help@smartsol.pro](mailto:help@smartsol.pro)
- WhatsApp: [+7 771 546 98 68](https://wa.me/77715469868), [+7 708 188 63 56](https://wa.me/77081886356)

We process data under the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Personal Data and Its Protection”.

## What data we receive

When you send a request on the website:

- your name;
- your phone number;
- your company name, if you provide it;
- a description of the task;
- the address and title of the page the request was sent from.

Along with the request, the website passes on where you came from:

- ad tags (UTM tags, fbclid, gclid, yclid);
- the first page of your visit and the site you came from.

Technical data: IP address, browser and device type. The website hosting and the form service see this data.

## Why

- To contact you about your request and discuss the task.
- To prepare a proposal and a contract.
- To understand which ads bring customers and stop spending on ads that don’t work.
- To protect the form from spam.

## Legal basis

Your consent. You give it when you tick “I agree to the collection and processing of personal data” before sending the form. You can’t send a request without consent, but you can always message us on WhatsApp or by email.

## Where the data is processed

- **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.): website hosting. The servers are located outside Kazakhstan.
- **Web3Forms**: the form service. The request passes through it and reaches us as an email at help@smartsol.pro.
- **Email** help@smartsol.pro: this is where we read and store requests.
- **WhatsApp**: if you message us there.

Some of these services store data outside Kazakhstan. By sending a request, you agree to such cross-border transfer.

## How long we keep it

We keep the data while it is needed to work on your request. If you withdraw consent, we delete it.

## Your rights

You can:

- find out what data of yours we hold;
- get a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- ask us to delete the data;
- withdraw your consent to processing.

## How to withdraw consent or delete your data

Write to [help@smartsol.pro](mailto:help@smartsol.pro) or on WhatsApp [+7 771 546 98 68](https://wa.me/77715469868). Give the name and phone number you left in the request. We will delete the data and let you know.

## How we protect data

The website works over a secure HTTPS connection. Only the staff who handle requests have access to them.

## Changes to this policy

The new version of the policy is published on this page. Effective from the date of publication on the website.
