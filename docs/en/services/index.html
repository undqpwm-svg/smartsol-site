Services

We take on tasks where accounting, sales and marketing need to work together. Each service can be ordered separately.

Free task review Message us on WhatsApp

1C: customisation and implementation

We implement and customise 1C:Accounting, Trade Management, ERP and Payroll for Kazakhstan. We build reports and print forms, new documents, modules and extensions. We customise through extensions, so the configuration can be updated without trouble.

What you get: 1C does what your business needs, not the other way round.

Integrations

We connect 1C with your website, CRM (Bitrix24, amoCRM and custom ones), banks, Kaspi, Wolt, Halyk Market, Forte Market, i-⁠teka.kz, provizor.kz, the National Product Catalogue, the e-invoicing system and Google Sheets. We set up automatic exchange of stock, prices, orders and payments.

What you get: data appears where it is needed, without manual entry or errors.

1C audit and accounting recovery

We check the database and settings and find errors in stock, settlements and taxes. We restore neglected accounting and set it up so the problem does not come back.

What you get: accounting you can trust.

Websites and online stores

We build landing pages, corporate websites and online stores with payment via Kaspi, Halyk, Freedom, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We connect them to 1C: products, stock and orders stay in sync.

What you get: a website that sells and needs no manual work.

Mobile apps

Apps for customers, employees and managers: orders, reports, approvals. With data exchange with 1C and CRM.

Bots and AI assistants

Bots on WhatsApp and Telegram that answer customers around the clock, give advice, issue invoices, take orders and pass them to 1C or CRM. Difficult questions go to a manager.

What you get: not a single request gets lost at night or at the weekend.

Marketing

We bring in customers and help you sell:

  • Targeted ads on Instagram and Facebook: setup, management, reports on cost per lead.
  • SMM: running Instagram and Threads, content plan, posts.
  • Content: reels, carousels, voiced videos, design in your brand style.
  • Branding: logo, corporate identity, brand book.
  • Link to sales: leads from ads go straight into CRM, a bot or 1C, and you see which ads bring in money.

What you get: marketing with measurable results.

Ongoing support

Subscription service for 1C and our solutions: updates, improvements, advice, bug fixes. Convenient when tasks come up regularly.
